Why SmartEntry

The problem with manual visitor management

Paper-based or poorly integrated processes make visitor records harder to manage, retrieve, and monitor.

01

Slow registration

Manual intake creates delays at entry points and increases staff workload.

02

Limited tracking

Entry and exit activity can be difficult to monitor consistently across the organization.

03

Poor record retrieval

Visitor history and older records can be difficult to find when records are physical or fragmented.

04

Weak reporting

Manual systems provide limited visibility for dashboards, trends, and operational reports.

05

Incident gaps

Security incidents may be documented inconsistently, reducing accountability and follow-up.

06

Physical dependency

Heavy reliance on paper records increases management and storage challenges.

How it works

A simple end-to-end workflow

The proposed system is structured around an easy operational flow for security teams, hosts, and administrators.

Register visitor

Capture visitor details and associate the visit with a host.

Check in

Record arrival and establish an active visitor session.

Monitor & report

Track visitor activity and document any security incident.

Check out

Close the visit and retain searchable history for future review.

Security & privacy

Protecting visitor information

The proposal includes encryption, hashing, secure transport, and privacy-focused information sharing.

Data protection

  • AES-256 encryption in transit.
  • SHA-3 hashing of IDs.
  • TLS 1.3 enforcement.
  • Zero-knowledge model to protect privacy.

Incident intelligence network

Beyond visitor logging, SmartEntry proposes a shared intelligence model for detecting and communicating security risks.

  • Fake identification
  • Unauthorized access
  • Theft
  • Misconduct
  • Blacklisted visitors
Shared alerts can include incident category, date, organization name, and risk level instead of directly sharing personal information.
